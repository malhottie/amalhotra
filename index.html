About

I earned my MD at the Frank H. Netter MD School of Medicine at Quinnipiac University in 2026, where I completed the Medical Education Concentration and served as class president. Since May 2026 I have worked in the Functional and Reconstructive Urology program at CU Anschutz with Brian J. Flynn, MD, coordinating device trials, writing protocols, and producing surgical video.

Before urology I worked on health services research in orthopedics at NYU Langone, and before that on supramolecular chemistry at Cal State Long Beach. Outside the hospital I forge and grind knives, build furniture, restore vintage audio equipment, and cook.

Research

4 areas

Male stress urinary incontinence

Who does well after bladder outlet surgery, and why.

  • Long-term patient-reported outcomes after male SUI surgery (protocol author)
  • A clinical complexity and risk grading system for bladder outlet surgery
  • ProACT adjustable continence therapy: quality of life, sexual function, lateral-view fluoroscopy for balloon placement

Device trials

Study coordinator for multicenter industry trials.

  • ACT for female SUI, IDE trial
  • ProACT Post-Approval Study (NCT03767595)

Urethral stricture and reconstruction

Options after a urethroplasty fails.

  • Paclitaxel drug-coated balloon dilation for recurrent stricture after urethroplasty
  • Transcorporal artificial urinary sphincter technique manual

Patients and the care they avoid

Health services questions, from orthopedics to urology.

  • Medical mistrust and vicarious experience in BPH treatment avoidance (VA)
  • Patient satisfaction and surgeon volume in total shoulder arthroplasty
